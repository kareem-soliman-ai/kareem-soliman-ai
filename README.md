# Kareem Soliman

Independent researcher working on a recurring problem across statistics, mechanistic interpretability, clinical AI, and evaluation: what looks like noise is often structure we have not learned to read yet.

My background is in organisational psychology, measurement, and applied research. I work across measurement error correction, reasoning geometry in transformers, uncertainty-aware inference, psychometric evaluation for AI systems, and relationship-first computational design.

## Current Research Themes

- **Structured variance as generative information**: treating error, uncertainty, and failure modes as signals rather than discardable noise.
- **Mechanistic interpretability**: understanding when models are actually reasoning instead of shortcutting.
- **Measurement and evaluation**: applying psychometrics and measurement theory to AI behaviour and scientific inference.
- **Uncertainty reasoning**: building systems that operate well when the hypothesis space itself is moving.

## Selected Work

- **[Trajectory Geometry](https://github.com/kareem-soliman-ai/trajectory-geometry-public)**  
  Geometric signatures of reasoning in transformer hidden states, including dimensional collapse in failure and explore-then-commit dynamics.

- **[PsychScope](https://github.com/kareem-soliman-ai/PsychScope)**  
  An open-source measurement framework for evaluating AI constructs with psychometric discipline rather than benchmark theatre.

- **Probability Cloud Regression**  
  A measurement-error framework for recovering true relationships when both variables are noisy. Public companion materials are being prepared.

- **Aurora**  
  A reasoning system for domains where the hypothesis space evolves as evidence arrives. Public-facing research artifacts are in preparation.

- **Relationship-Oriented Programming Language (NEXUS)**  
  A programming language built around relationships as first-class computational objects rather than treating them as secondary metadata.

## Why This Work Connects

Across these projects, the same move keeps showing up: hidden structure lives in the part most workflows flatten away. Measurement error, hidden-state geometry, diagnostic uncertainty, evaluation variance, and relational tension are not nuisances around the real signal. They are often the real signal.
