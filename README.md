# Kareem Soliman

Independent AI researcher based in Sydney, working on measurement and translation systems for AI-era decision-making.

My work asks a recurring question: what hidden structure becomes visible when we stop treating uncertainty, measurement error, evaluator variance, and reasoning failure as disposable noise?

That question connects my projects across measurement error correction, AI evaluation, organisational capability discovery, reasoning geometry, dynamic hypothesis management, and research-to-market translation.

## Main Project Families

### Aletheia / Probability Cloud Regression
Error-as-information methods for modelling structured measurement uncertainty. PCR is the statistical core; Aletheia is the broader pipeline for variance forensics, error-source modelling, structural analysis, and multi-agent interpretation.

### Criterion Labs
Psychometrically disciplined AI evaluation. Focus areas include evaluator-quality auditing for LLM-as-judge systems, construct-valid benchmark design, deterministic scoring, and bespoke capability evaluation.

### OrgLens / Executive Interview System
Asynchronous executive interviewing for organisational AI capability discovery. EIS converts executive knowledge into capability maps, workflow opportunity maps, and benchmark seeds for organisation-specific AI evaluation.

### Veridex
Research-to-market intelligence. Veridex turns frontier research into claim-governed opportunity briefs, stakeholder maps, and commercialisation hypotheses.

### Trajectory Geometry
Mechanistic interpretability work on geometric signatures of reasoning and failure in transformer hidden states.

### Aurora
Dynamic hypothesis management and uncertainty-aware reasoning over evolving possibility spaces, with clinical, scientific, and decision-support research applications.

### Lemma
An ADHD-aware learning companion for mathematics, statistics, Python, and AI research foundations.

### Parallax
Multi-agent problem formulation and civilisation-scale reasoning architecture.

## Why This Work Connects

The unifying thesis is that many systems fail because they flatten the wrong thing.

Measurement error is flattened into nuisance variance.
LLM judge disagreement is flattened into average preference.
Executive knowledge is flattened into workshop notes.
Clinical uncertainty is flattened into fixed diagnostic categories.
Model reasoning is flattened into final answers.
Research is flattened into papers without implementation pathways.

My work builds systems that preserve, measure, and use those structures instead.
